# Атмосферные звуки

- `wind.ogg` — «Wind Loop» by AntumDeluge, based on InspectorJ, **CC BY 3.0**. https://opengameart.org/content/wind-loop
- `fire.m4a` — «Fireplace Sound loop» by PagDev, **CC0**. https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop
- `tavern.ogg` — «Crowd Shouting/Speaking Ambience» by StarNinjas, **CC0**. https://opengameart.org/content/crowd-shoutingspeaking-ambience
- `cave.ogg` — «Loopable Dungeon Ambience» by JaggedStone, **CC0**. https://opengameart.org/content/loopable-dungeon-ambience
